Research

Research & publications

My research focuses on reasoning, knowledge acquisition, and adaptation in intelligent systems. I am particularly interested in how models learn beyond their prior knowledge, form new abstractions, and adapt their reasoning to unfamiliar environments.

Research interests

Reasoning

How models reason beyond their prior knowledge and form new abstractions.

Knowledge acquisition

How models learn new knowledge and integrate it into their reasoning.

Adaptation

How intelligent systems adapt their reasoning to unfamiliar environments.
