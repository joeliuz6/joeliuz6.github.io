I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at the **University of Rochester**, advised by [Prof. Hangfeng He](https://hornhehhf.github.io/).

My research focuses on **reasoning, representation learning, knowledge acquisition, and adaptation in intelligent systems**. I am particularly interested in how models learn beyond their prior knowledge, form new abstractions, and adapt their reasoning to unfamiliar environments.

Before starting my Ph.D., I received an M.S. in Computer Engineering from the University of Rochester, where I worked with [Prof. Tony Geng](https://www.tonytgeng.com/) on hardware–software co-design. I received my B.Eng. in Automation from South China University of Technology.
